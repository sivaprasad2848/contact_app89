Classical AI -(Artificial Intelligenece)
----------------------------------------
AI(Artificial Intelligence):- Machine mimic like humans.

Intelligence:- The ability of a living being to take decision in a particular 
context what action to be taken


Intelligence is the ability of a living being to perceive its environment, 
understand a given context, make decisions,and choose appropriate 
actions to achieve a goal.


Perception → Understanding → Reasoning → Decision → Action → Learning

Perception: Detects fire and heat.
Understanding: Realizes there is danger.
Reasoning: Determines that staying there is unsafe.
Decision: Decides to leave.
Action: Moves away and possibly alerts others.
Learning: Remembers what happened and may react faster next time.

“Intelligence is the ability to understand a situation, 
make an appropriate decision, and take action toward a goal.
